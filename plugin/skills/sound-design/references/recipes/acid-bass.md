# Acid Bass

**Use:** the squelchy, resonant TB-303 line — acid house, techno, electro. The character is a single oscillator through a highly-resonant low-pass whose cutoff is swept by a fast envelope, played with slides and accents.

**Start from:** [Polysynth](../devices/polysynth.md) (single saw or square oscillator) — see the guide. Polymer works too; the 303 sound is about the filter, not the oscillator engine.

**Build:**
1. One oscillator — saw (brighter, more classic acid) or square (hollower, rounder). Keep it simple; the 303 has a single oscillator.
2. Filter to low-pass with **high resonance** — this is the defining move. Enough resonance that the peak sings, short of self-oscillation.
3. Route a filter/modulation envelope to the filter Frequency with a fast attack and short decay, so each note "wows" open then snaps shut — the squelch. This envelope, not the amp, makes the acid movement (see [../devices/polysynth.md](../devices/polysynth.md) on the mod envelope).
4. Amp envelope tight: fast attack, short-ish decay, moderate sustain so notes are punchy and separated.
5. Add glide/portamento so legato notes slide (the 303 slide) — see [../../../coach/references/modulators.md](../../../coach/references/modulators.md).
6. Chain: Saturator/distortion (drive is essential — a clean acid line is limp; overdrive gives the screaming edge) → optional Delay+ (short synced repeats for space). See [../fx-character.md](../fx-character.md) on saturation before vs after the filter — drive *before* the low-pass keeps the grit controlled.

**Parameter targets:** Filter low-pass ~25-45% resting, resonance high (~70-90%). Mod-envelope amount to cutoff high, envelope attack ~0%, decay short-to-medium (this sets how long each squelch lasts). Saturator drive moderate-to-high.

**Variations:** brighter/screamier (raise resonance and mod-envelope depth, open the resting cutoff); dirtier (more drive, harder distortion device — confirm its live name); dubbier (more Delay+ feedback with darkened repeats).

**Descriptors it hits:** [aggressive](../descriptor-dictionary.md#aggressive), [gritty](../descriptor-dictionary.md#gritty), [bright](../descriptor-dictionary.md#bright) (see [descriptor-dictionary](../descriptor-dictionary.md)).
