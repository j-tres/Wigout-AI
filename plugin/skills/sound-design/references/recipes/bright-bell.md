# Bright Bell

**Use:** a clear, ringing bell/mallet/glockenspiel tone — melodic bells, chimes, music-box lines, and the shimmering top layer of a hook. Bright, glassy, inharmonic, with a struck attack that rings out.

**Start from:** [FM-4](../devices/fm-4.md) — the classic bell machine: a carrier modulated by an operator at a **non-integer ratio** produces the clangy inharmonic partials a filter can never make. See the guide. [Phase-4](../devices/phase-4.md) with non-integer pitch relationships is the equally-valid alternative.

**Build:**
1. Carrier at ratio 1, modulated by an operator at a **non-integer ratio** — the inharmonicity is what makes it read as a bell rather than a pluck (see [../devices/fm-4.md](../devices/fm-4.md) and [glassy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#glassy)).
2. Put a **fast-attack, medium-decay, low-sustain envelope on the modulation depth** so the metallic clang strikes hard and rings out into a purer tone — the struck-bell contour.
3. Amp envelope: fast attack, long-ish release so the bell rings after the key is up; low/no sustain plateau so it decays naturally like a struck object.
4. Keep the filter open/bright — the tone should be forward and glassy, not rolled off.
5. Chain: EQ+ (a high-shelf boost for top-end sheen; trim low-mid warmth so the highs dominate — see [bright](../descriptor-dictionary.md#bright)) → Chorus+ (a little shimmer/width) → a bright, longer Reverb for the classic bell bloom. See [../fx-character.md](../fx-character.md).

**Parameter targets:** Modulator ratio non-integer; modulation depth moderate-high; its envelope attack ~0%, decay medium, sustain low. Amp attack ~0%, release long. Filter open. EQ high-shelf boost; small low-mid trim. Reverb tail longer, bright, mix moderate.

**Variations:** glassier/brighter (more modulation depth, higher shelf, brighter reverb — mind [harsh](../descriptor-dictionary.md#harsh)); softer mallet (less depth, shorter release, warmer EQ); music-box (shorter decay, add synced Delay+ for repeats).

**Descriptors it hits:** [glassy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#glassy), [bright](../descriptor-dictionary.md#bright), [airy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#airy) (see [descriptor-dictionary](../descriptor-dictionary.md)).
