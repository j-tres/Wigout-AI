# Sub Bass

**Use:** the clean, felt-not-heard foundation under a mix — a pure low fundamental that adds weight without clutter (house, pop, trap sub layer, anything that needs bottom).

**Start from:** Polymer with a sine or triangle source — see [../devices/polymer.md](../devices/polymer.md). [Polysynth](../devices/polysynth.md) with one oscillator on triangle/sine works equally well.

**Build:**
1. One oscillator, sine (purest, cleanest) or triangle (a hint more harmonic content so it is slightly more audible on small speakers). Play it in the low octaves.
2. Amp envelope: fast attack (so notes start on the beat), moderate-to-high sustain, short-ish release so consecutive notes don't overlap and mud up.
3. Filter mostly open — there is almost nothing to remove from a sine; a gentle low-pass only tames stray highs.
4. Keep it **mono and centered** — no chorus, no stereo width, no reverb. Width and space in the sub register cancel in mono and vanish on club systems (see the mono-bass caveat in [../fx-character.md](../fx-character.md)).
5. Optional chain: a light Saturator to add a low harmonic so the pitch reads on phones/laptops → EQ+ high-pass below ~30 Hz to clear inaudible rumble, tiny bell boost ~50-80 Hz for weight.

**Parameter targets:** Filter low-pass high/open (~80-100%), resonance at/near zero (resonance here just adds an uncontrolled boom). Amp attack low, sustain high, release short. Saturator drive very low if used at all.

**Variations:** triangle instead of sine for a touch more presence; add a single higher harmonic layer (octave-up saw, low level, gently filtered) so the line is audible on small speakers while the sine carries the weight; sidechain the sub to the kick (engineer's domain) so they share the low end.

**Descriptors it hits:** [boomy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#boomy), [warm](../descriptor-dictionary.md#warm) (see [descriptor-dictionary](../descriptor-dictionary.md)).
