# Noise Percussion

**Use:** synthesized percussion from filtered noise — hi-hats, shakers, snare/clap top-end, risers, and FX hits. When you want a hat that fits the track exactly rather than a sampled one, you build it from a noise burst shaped by a filter and a fast envelope.

**Start from:** [Polymer](../devices/polymer.md) with a noise source in the oscillator slot (confirm the slot exposes noise live), or [Sampler](../devices/sampler.md) loaded with a noise/found-sound one-shot (loop off). See the guides and [../synthesis-fundamentals.md](../synthesis-fundamentals.md) on noise as a source.

**Build:**
1. Source: noise (white/colored). Pitch is irrelevant — the *filter* and *envelope* make the instrument, not the note.
2. Shape the color with the filter: a **high-pass** (or band-pass) leaves only the top for hats/shakers; a low-pass/band-pass around the low-mids gives a snare/clap body. Resonance tunes a pitched ring into the noise.
3. Amp envelope is the whole rhythm: near-instant attack, very short decay/release for a closed hat; a longer decay for an open hat or a shaker; a slow *rising* attack over seconds for a riser/uplifter.
4. For a tuned/metallic edge, add a touch of resonance or ring-mod so the noise has a pitch center (see [../../../coach/references/modulators.md](../../../coach/references/modulators.md)).
5. Chain: Saturator (a little grit and density so it cuts) → EQ+ (shape the exact band; high-pass out any low thump you don't want) → short Reverb/Delay+ for space (great on claps). See [../fx-character.md](../fx-character.md).

**Parameter targets:** High-pass high for hats (only the top passes); band-pass mid for snare body. Resonance low unless you want a pitched ring. Amp attack ~0%, decay very short (closed hat) to medium (open hat/shaker); slow attack for risers. Saturator drive light-moderate.

**Variations:** closed vs open hat (decay short vs long); snare/clap (lower band-pass, add body + short reverb); riser (slow rising amp + slowly opening filter over a bar); metallic hat (add resonance/ring-mod — toward [glassy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#glassy)).

**Descriptors it hits:** [airy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#airy), [punchy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#punchy), [gritty](../descriptor-dictionary.md#gritty) (see [descriptor-dictionary](../descriptor-dictionary.md)).
