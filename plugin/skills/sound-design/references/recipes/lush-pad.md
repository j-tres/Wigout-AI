# Lush Pad

**Use:** a rich, wide, evolving pad that blooms over time — the big cinematic/dream-pop bed with ensemble movement and depth. More motion and stereo than the [warm-pad](warm-pad.md); this one is the centerpiece, not the background.

**Start from:** [Polymer](../devices/polymer.md) or [Polysynth](../devices/polysynth.md) with a saw source and **unison/detune** — see the guides.

**Build:**
1. Unison saws with a moderate detune spread — an ensemble of slightly-out-of-tune voices is the root of "lush" (see [lush](../descriptor-dictionary.md#lush)).
2. Filter to low-pass, moderate — open enough to shimmer, not so open it turns harsh. A little resonance.
3. Amp envelope: slow attack, high sustain, long release so it swells and the tail blooms into reverb.
4. Movement is essential: a slow LFO on pitch/detune, pulse width, or wavetable position gives the gentle evolving drift (see [../../../coach/references/modulators.md](../../../coach/references/modulators.md)). Keep it subtle and slow.
5. Chain: Chorus+ (stereo depth and width — low depth, slow rate) → EQ+ (creative shaping; gentle high-shelf air) → Reverb (long, lush tail, longish pre-delay so the attack stays defined before it blooms). See [../fx-character.md](../fx-character.md) on "space" and "width". Do not pile reverb + detune until it washes into [muddy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#muddy).

**Parameter targets:** Unison voices moderate-high, detune moderate. Filter low-pass ~45-65%, resonance low. Amp attack slow, release long. Chorus+ depth low, rate slow, mix modest. Reverb pre-delay lengthened, tail long, mix moderate.

**Variations:** wider/dreamier (more Chorus+ and detune, brighter shelf); darker/warmer (lower the low-pass, shorter/darker reverb — toward [warm-pad](warm-pad.md)); more evolving (deeper/slower LFO, add a second slow modulator on a different target); octave layer underneath for weight.

**Descriptors it hits:** [lush](../descriptor-dictionary.md#lush), [wide](../descriptor-dictionary.md#wide), [airy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#airy) (see [descriptor-dictionary](../descriptor-dictionary.md)).
