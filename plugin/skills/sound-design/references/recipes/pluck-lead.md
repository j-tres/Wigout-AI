# Pluck Lead

**Use:** a short, bright, percussive melodic lead — the plucky synth line in pop, future-bass, and deep house that rings for a moment then gets out of the way. Great for arpeggios and delay-driven riffs.

**Start from:** [Polymer](../devices/polymer.md) (bright oscillator + filter envelope) — see the guide. [Polysynth](../devices/polysynth.md) works identically; the pluck is made by the filter envelope, not the engine.

**Build:**
1. Bright source (saw or a bright wavetable position) so there is upper content for the filter to open onto.
2. The pluck move: route a **filter/modulation envelope** to the filter Frequency with a fast attack and a short decay to a low sustain — the cutoff snaps open on the transient then closes, the "wow" that reads as a pluck (see [../devices/polymer.md](../devices/polymer.md) on the filter envelope).
3. Amp envelope: fast attack, short-to-medium decay, low-ish sustain, short release — the loudness should also fall away so the note is brief.
4. Modest resonance adds a little bell to the pluck; too much whistles.
5. Chain: EQ+ (light high-pass to keep it clear of the bass) → synced Delay+ (a dotted-eighth ping is the signature pluck-riff move — darken the repeats so they sit behind the dry note) → Reverb (short, for a little air). See [../fx-character.md](../fx-character.md) on time effects last / on a send.

**Parameter targets:** Filter mod-envelope amount moderate-high, envelope attack ~0%, decay short-to-medium (sets pluck length), sustain low. Amp attack ~0%, decay/release short. Resonance modest. Delay+ dotted-1/8, feedback modest, repeats darkened.

**Variations:** brighter/glassier (raise cutoff and mod-env depth, add a touch of FM/phase source); softer (round the amp attack slightly, lower resonance); longer tail (more Delay+ feedback and Reverb for an ambient pluck).

**Descriptors it hits:** [punchy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#punchy), [bright](../descriptor-dictionary.md#bright), [glassy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#glassy) (see [descriptor-dictionary](../descriptor-dictionary.md)).
