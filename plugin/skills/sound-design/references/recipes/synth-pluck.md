# Synth Pluck

**Use:** a short, percussive, tuned pluck for arps, rhythmic stabs, and melodic ostinatos — deep house, future-bass, pop. Cousin of the [pluck-lead](pluck-lead.md): where that one is a melodic lead, this is a rhythmic building-block that repeats under a track.

**Start from:** [Polymer](../devices/polymer.md) (fast filter envelope) for a rounded pluck, or [Phase-4](../devices/phase-4.md) for a metallic, slightly-inharmonic pluck. See the guides.

**Build:**
1. Source: a saw/wavetable for a warm pluck, or Phase-4 with a short burst of phase-mod for a dinky, metallic attack that settles clean (see [../devices/phase-4.md](../devices/phase-4.md) on putting an envelope on the phase-mod amount).
2. The pluck: a fast-attack, short-decay envelope to the filter Frequency (or to the phase-mod amount) so the note snaps bright then closes. Short is the point — a pluck is defined by how quickly it dies.
3. Amp envelope: fast attack, short decay, low sustain, short release — tight and percussive (see [punchy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#punchy)).
4. A little resonance adds a bell-tone to the tail.
5. Chain: EQ+ (high-pass to keep it out of the bass) → synced Delay+ (rhythmic repeats build the pattern — a dotted-eighth or triplet feel) → short Reverb for glue. Time effects last / on a send (see [../fx-character.md](../fx-character.md)).

**Parameter targets:** Filter/phase-mod envelope amount moderate-high, attack ~0%, decay short (this sets pluck length), sustain low. Amp attack ~0%, decay/release short. Resonance modest. Delay+ synced, feedback modest, repeats darkened.

**Variations:** metallic (Phase-4 source, non-integer pitch, envelope on phase-mod — toward [glassy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#glassy)); warmer (lower cutoff, saw source, less resonance); longer/dreamier (lengthen the decay and Delay+/Reverb for an ambient pluck).

**Descriptors it hits:** [punchy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#punchy), [bright](../descriptor-dictionary.md#bright), [glassy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#glassy) (see [descriptor-dictionary](../descriptor-dictionary.md)).
