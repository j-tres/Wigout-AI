# Reese Bass

**Use:** DnB/dubstep detuned growl bass — a moving, hollow, aggressive mid-bass that sits under a track and snarls.

**Start from:** Polymer (detuned saw source) — see [../devices/polymer.md](../devices/polymer.md). For a grindier, more metallic Reese, [Phase-4](../devices/phase-4.md) with moderate phase-mod between two detuned oscillators is the classic alternative.

**Build:**
1. Two (or three) detuned saw oscillators — unison with a slight detune spread. The beating between them *is* the Reese; without detune it is just a saw bass.
2. Low-pass the filter into the mid-range and add modest resonance; the growl comes from **moving** the cutoff — automate it, or assign a slow LFO to the filter Frequency (see [../../../coach/references/modulators.md](../../../coach/references/modulators.md)).
3. Amp envelope: fast attack, high sustain, medium release — it needs to be a held, droning body, not a pluck.
4. Chain: Saturator (grit and harmonic density so the filtered mids bite) → EQ+ (scoop boxy low-mids ~250-400 Hz, high-pass the sub) → Chorus+ (width on the mids only). **Keep the sub mono** — split a clean mono sub layer below ~100-120 Hz and only widen the top. See [../fx-character.md](../fx-character.md).

**Parameter targets:** Detune subtle-to-moderate (too much smears pitch). Filter low-pass ~35-50%, resonance modest. LFO to cutoff slow (sync to a bar or half-bar) for the growl sweep. Saturator drive moderate-to-high. EQ low-mid scoop a few dB, narrow-ish.

**Variations:** brighter (open the low-pass, raise the LFO's ceiling); harder (more Saturator drive, add resonance); wider (unison voices up, more Chorus+ on the mids — never the sub).

**Descriptors it hits:** [aggressive](../descriptor-dictionary.md#aggressive), [wide](../descriptor-dictionary.md#wide), [gritty](../descriptor-dictionary.md#gritty) (see [descriptor-dictionary](../descriptor-dictionary.md)).
