# Electric Piano

**Use:** a Rhodes/Wurlitzer-style electric piano — the bell-and-tine tone of soul, neo-soul, jazz, and ballads. A percussive inharmonic "tine" attack over a warm, hollow body that responds to how hard you play.

**Start from:** [FM-4](../devices/fm-4.md) — the textbook use of FM: a modulator with a percussive envelope creates the tine attack that decays into a purer tone. See the guide. (A good EP multisample in [Sampler](../devices/sampler.md) is the realism alternative.)

**Build:**
1. Carrier near ratio 1 (the fundamental pitch), modulated by an operator that gives the metallic tine. Modest modulation depth for a mellow EP, more for a barky, aggressive one (see [../devices/fm-4.md](../devices/fm-4.md) on ratio and modulation depth).
2. The tine: put a **percussive envelope on the modulator's level** — fast attack, short decay — so the bright inharmonic attack strikes and then fades into the warmer sustained carrier. This is the essence of the sound.
3. A little Feedback adds body/growl; keep it low for a clean EP.
4. Amp envelope: fast attack, medium decay, moderate sustain, medium release — struck but singing.
5. Make it velocity-sensitive: harder playing should raise the modulation depth (brighter tine) — the classic EP dynamic. Confirm the live routing.
6. Chain: Chorus+ (the signature Rhodes stereo shimmer — low depth, slow rate) → EQ+ (gentle presence, roll off any fizz) → light Reverb (a room, not a hall) → optional tremolo/auto-pan and a light Saturator for a driven Wurli. See [../fx-character.md](../fx-character.md).

**Parameter targets:** Carrier ratio ~1; modulator ratio integer for a focused tine (non-integer for a clangier, bell-ier EP). Modulation depth modest, its envelope attack ~0% / decay short. Feedback low. Amp attack ~0%, decay medium, sustain moderate. Chorus+ depth low, rate slow.

**Variations:** brighter/barky (more modulation depth and velocity range — toward [glassy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#glassy)); mellower/darker (less depth, low-pass a little); driven Wurlitzer (add Saturator and tremolo). For the dusty-tape version see [lofi-keys](lofi-keys.md).

**Descriptors it hits:** [warm](../descriptor-dictionary.md#warm), [glassy](../descriptor-dictionary.md#glassy), [bright](../descriptor-dictionary.md#bright) (see [descriptor-dictionary](../descriptor-dictionary.md)).
