# Genre Recipes

Ten starting points. Each gives a tempo, a drum skeleton in **step-grid
terms** (16 steps/bar at 1/16; step 1 = downbeat, 5 = beat 2, 9 = beat 3,
13 = beat 4), a bass approach, harmony tendency, signature instrumentation,
and stock-Bitwig device picks. These are launch pads — break them on
purpose once the groove sits. Read project tempo/key before applying.

## House — 120-126 BPM
- **Kick**: 1, 5, 9, 13 (four-on-the-floor). **Clap/snare**: 5, 13.
  **Open hat**: 3, 7, 11, 15 (the offbeat "tss"). **Closed hat**: all 16.
- **Bass**: offbeat plucks landing on 3, 7, 11, 15, or a rolling 1/8 line
  under the kick; sidechain to the kick for pump.
- **Harmony**: ii–V–I and vi–IV–I–V loops, 7th/9th pads, warm minor.
- **Instruments**: pad stabs, filtered chords, disco-ish plucks.
- **Bitwig**: Polymer (bass + stabs), Drum Machine with v1 Kick / v1 Clap /
  v1 Hat, EQ+ and Sweep for filter automation, Compressor for pump.

## Techno — 130-145 BPM
- **Kick**: 1, 5, 9, 13, long and dominant. **Hat**: offbeats 3,7,11,15,
  or relentless 1/16. **Clap**: 5, 13, drenched in reverb. Ride the hats.
- **Bass**: one hypnotic note, rumbling sub tied to the kick tail.
- **Harmony**: minimal — a drone, one minor stab, tension from timbre not
  chords. Modulate the filter, not the notes.
- **Instruments**: industrial stabs, noise sweeps, metallic percussion.
- **Bitwig**: Polymer or FM-4 (stab/bass), Ladder or Comb filter, Resonator
  Bank for metallic hits, Reverb + Delay-4 for cavernous space.

## Lo-Fi Hip-Hop — 70-90 BPM
- **Kick**: 1, 11 (or 1, 7). **Snare**: 5, 13, laid back — nudge +10-20ms
  late for drag. **Hat**: 1/8 or dotted, swing 55-62%. Keep it loose.
- **Bass**: sparse, round, root-of-chord on the 1, walk to the next chord.
- **Harmony**: jazzy maj7/min7/9ths, ii–V–I, chromatic passing chords.
- **Instruments**: dusty Rhodes/piano, vinyl crackle, mellow guitar.
- **Bitwig**: Sampler (chopped chords), Organ or Polysynth (Rhodes-ish),
  Bit-8 + EQ+ (low-pass ~8kHz) for lo-fi grit, Delay+ for tape echo.

## Drum & Bass — 170-176 BPM
- **Break**: the "amen" 2-step — **Kick**: 1, 11. **Snare**: 5, 13 (plus
  ghost snares on 7, 15). **Hat**: fast 1/16 with gaps; chop and shuffle.
- **Bass**: sub (Reese) on the root, long and moving; half-time feel under
  a double-time break.
- **Harmony**: dark minor pads, one or two chords, cinematic.
- **Instruments**: chopped breakbeat, Reese bass, atmospheric pads.
- **Bitwig**: Sampler (break chopping), Polymer/FM-4 with unison + Ladder
  (Reese), Freq Shifter for movement, Reverb for the pad wash.

## Trap — 130-150 BPM (half-time feel, ~70-75)
- **Kick**: 1, and syncopated 7 or 11. **Snare/clap**: 9 only (backbeat on
  beat 3 = the half-time signature). **Hat**: 1/16 with **rolls** — burst
  to 1/32 or triplets on 15-16 for the signature stutter.
- **Bass**: 808 — pitched, gliding between root notes, long sustain.
- **Harmony**: dark minor, single sustained chord or ominous melody.
- **Instruments**: 808, bells, plucked leads, orchestral hits.
- **Bitwig**: FM-4 or Phase-4 (808 with pitch glide), Drum Machine (v1 Hat
  for rolls), Distortion on the 808, EQ+ to carve kick/808 overlap.

## Ambient — 50-75 BPM (or pulseless)
- **Drums**: often none. If present: soft kick on 1, brushed texture, no
  strict grid. Let clip lengths drift against each other.
- **Bass**: a low drone, or nothing — let pads own the bottom.
- **Harmony**: slow-moving, suspended and add9 chords, modal (Dorian,
  Lydian), long crossfades between two chords.
- **Instruments**: evolving pads, field recordings, granular textures.
- **Bitwig**: Polysynth or Poly Grid (evolving pads), Sampler in granular
  mode, Reverb (long tail), Delay-4, Resonator Bank for drone harmonics.

## Synthwave — 100-120 BPM
- **Kick**: 1, 5, 9, 13. **Snare**: 5, 13 with big gated reverb. **Hat**:
  1/16 driving, or 1/8 offbeat. Add tom fills every 8 bars.
- **Bass**: arpeggiated 1/16 root-fifth-octave, or a pulsing 1/8 synth bass.
- **Harmony**: minor with nostalgic vi–IV–I–V, sus chords, bright leads.
- **Instruments**: analog-style saw leads, gated-reverb drums, arps.
- **Bitwig**: Polysynth (saw lead + bass), Arpeggiator on the bass track,
  Chorus+ and Phaser+ for width, Reverb (gated via Gate after) on snare.

## Pop — 100-125 BPM
- **Kick**: 1, 9 (plus 7 for bounce). **Snare/clap**: 5, 13. **Hat**: 1/16
  with velocity accents on offbeats. Keep it clean and punchy.
- **Bass**: follows the kick, root notes, tight and simple.
- **Harmony**: I–V–vi–IV and vi–IV–I–V, diatonic, one memorable hook.
- **Instruments**: piano/synth chords, vocal-forward, plucky top line.
- **Bitwig**: Polymer (bass + pluck), Sampler (piano multisample), Drum
  Machine, Compressor + Peak Limiter for loudness, EQ+ for vocal space.

## Rock — 110-140 BPM
- **Kick**: 1, 9 (add 7, 11 for drive). **Snare**: 5, 13 hard on the
  backbeat. **Hat/ride**: steady 1/8, open on accents. Play fills, don't
  quantize them to death — humanize timing.
- **Bass**: locks to kick, root-driven, eighth-note pulse.
- **Harmony**: I–IV–V, i–VII–VI, power-chord roots, pentatonic leads.
- **Instruments**: distorted guitars, live-feel drums, bass, organ.
- **Bitwig**: Sampler (guitar) into Amp + Distortion, Organ, Drum Machine
  with velocity humanization, Transient Control to punch up drums.

## Cinematic — 60-140 BPM (tempo serves the scene)
- **Drums**: sparse — big taiko/timpani hits on 1 and 9, building to
  ostinato 1/16 strings in action cues. Silence is a tool.
- **Bass**: low brass and contrabass doubling the root, slow swells.
- **Harmony**: minor and modal, i–VI–III–VII, suspensions, big cadences;
  dynamics come from orchestration (add instruments) not from the beat.
- **Instruments**: strings, brass, choir, percussion, piano.
- **Bitwig**: Sampler (orchestral multisamples), Polysynth (pad bed under
  live samples), Reverb (hall, long pre-delay), EQ+ to seat the low brass.

## Using these
Pick the genre, set tempo, build the drum skeleton on the step grid, then
choose harmony from `harmony-progressions.md` and shape the arc with
`arrangement-energy.md`. Device names above are stock Bitwig only — confirm
exact instrument picks against the coach's `devices-instruments.md`.
